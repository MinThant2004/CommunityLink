PROBLEM ANALYSIS

The Challenges in Current Social Networks

Mainstream social platforms fail professional creators, industry leaders, and learners in Myanmar.

01. THE PROBLEM
Information Chaos
  • Open, uncurated platforms suffer from spam, noisy feeds, and low signal-to-noise ratios.
  • High-value technical and commercial discussions are buried by generic engagement algorithms.
  • Knowledge seekers cannot easily locate structured, verified subject matter expertise.
02. THE DEFICIT
Identity Impersonation
  • Lack of formal credential verification invites impersonation and unvetted claims.
  • Industry leaders lack a protected, professional environment to engage members safely.
  • Communities have no objective peer-review matrix to measure creator credibility.
03. THE GAP
Unresponsive Consultations
  • Members seeking vital career/industry advice message leaders in general inboxes where messages remain ignored.
  • Zero accountability: No service SLA, response timeline commitment, or formal oversight.
  • Creators lack seamless tools to monetize consulting and mastermind circles.

Guaranteed Turnaround Times

ORGANIZATIONAL MODEL

Hierarchical Community Architecture: 3-Tier Taxonomy

Eliminating feed clutter with intentional 3-level content containment.

LEVEL 1
Main Communities (Hubs)

Macro Domain Umbrellas

  • Represents broad industry verticals (e.g., Technology & AI, Healthcare, Finance, Creative Arts).
  • Chartered by platform governors; sets top-level policies and standards.
  • Primary entry point for broad member discovery and navigation.
LEVEL 2
Sub-Communities (Nodes)

Specialized Knowledge Hubs

  • Focused disciplines (e.g., C# & .NET Architecture, Clinical Medicine, Venture Syndicates).
  • Hosts the Multi-Filter Feed across Posts, Working Groups, and People.
  • Interactive polls, articles, and luminary rosters.
LEVEL 3
Smart Working Groups (Rooms)

Action-Oriented Workrooms

  • Intimate mastermind teams, cohort classes, and technical project rooms.
  • Decoupled Public/Private visibility and Join Policies (Free / Paid via LinkDrops).
  • Group creator ownership enforcement (creator cannot leave own group).
  • Rich code snippets, text attachments, and verified member rosters.
Hierarchy Traversal: Community [1..*] → Sub-Community [1..*] → Group [1..*] → Posts / Polls Cascading RBAC • Zero Permission Leakage

CORE INNOVATION

Priority Consultations with Response SLA

Solving unresponsive consulting inboxes through declared turnaround commitments and admin oversight.

STAGE 1
SLA Declaration
  • Verified leaders set public turnaround commitments (e.g., Within 24 Hours, Within 48 Hours).
  • Leaders define consultation rates in LinkDrops.
  • Displayed transparently on the leader's public profile card.
STAGE 2
Consultation Commitment
  • Members engage verified mentors with clear turnaround expectations.
  • Direct priority messaging stream initiated between client and domain expert.
  • Accountability window enforced by platform professional standards.
STAGE 3
Admin SLA Moderation
  • If a leader breaches SLA commitments or remains unresponsive, members report via Member/SLA reports.
  • Admin reviews details and executes direct moderation actions: Give Warning, Deactivate Member, or Dismiss.
  • Leaders maintain verified badges and platform standing only by honoring declared SLAs.
Enforcement: Turnaround Tracking • Direct Admin Reports • Disciplinary Sanctions (Warning / Deactivation / Dismissal) Accountability • Protected Network
