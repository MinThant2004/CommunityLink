## Brand & Style

This design system conveys an elite, members-only atmosphere optimized for verified executive networking and high-trust leadership communities. The aesthetic balances architectural minimalism with high-end private club restraint—rendered entirely within a luminous, pristine white spectrum.

The visual style is characterized by:
- **Pristine White Minimalism:** Canvas and layered surfaces built on pure optical white, alabaster, and pale slate tones, rejecting dark interfaces in favor of bright, daylight clarity.
- **Restrained Metallics:** Measured applications of polished champagne gold, brushed brass, and deep metallic amber against deep charcoal typography.
- **Architectural Precision:** Razor-thin hairline dividing lines, generous structural negative space, and disciplined layout alignment reminiscent of Swiss high-finance publication and private client portals.
- **Discreet Authority:** Subtle transitions, low-opacity warm ambient shadows, and an absence of uncalibrated bright primary colors or aggressive visual noise.

## Colors

The color palette anchors high-density contrast while retaining a warm, luxurious glow through secondary metallic accents.

### Palette Architecture
- **Surfaces & Canvases:**
  - Base Background: `#F8FAFC` (Alabaster slate)
  - Surface Pure: `#FFFFFF` (Pristine optical white for cards, sheets, elevated panels)
  - Surface Inset / Subdued: `#F1F5F9` (Muted platinum for tags, input wells, and segmented controls)
- **Typographic Hierarchy & Primary Anchors:**
  - Primary Slate: `#0F172A` (Deep executive charcoal; primary copy, commanding headers)
  - Body Slate: `#334155` (Sub-headers, longform content, primary icons)
  - Muted Charcoal: `#64748B` (Secondary descriptions, metadata, inactive states)
  - Subtle Ash: `#94A3B8` (Placeholder text, de-emphasized icons)
- **Metallics & Badges:**
  - Amber Gold (Secondary): `#B45309` (Active indicators, verified emblems, focus states)
  - Champagne Gold (Tertiary): `#C69214` (Executive highlights, badges, premium tiering)
  - Pale Champagne Wash: `#FEF3C7` (Badge background fills, selected row states)
- **Separators & Boundaries:**
  - Hairline Border: `#E2E8F0` (Default panel borders, subtle list splitters)
  - Strong Border: `#CBD5E1` (Interactive element borders, active states)

## Typography

Typography pairs the structural geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans for executive headlines with the functional precision of Inter for high-density transactional text and metrics.

- **Headlines:** Use Plus Jakarta Sans with tighter tracking to produce an editorial, authoritative stance without appearing decorative.
- **Body & Data:** Use Inter for effortless legibility across directories, portfolios, and message streams.
- **Uppercased Accents:** Use `caption-caps` exclusively for section categorizers, status badges, verification labels, and financial identifiers. Always set with wide tracking (`0.08em`) to enforce an elevated tone.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column grid on desktop, scaling to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column structure on mobile devices.

- **Grid Framework:**
  - Desktop (1280px and above): Max content container of 1360px centered, 2.5rem margins, 1.5rem gutters.
  - Tablet (768px - 1279px): Fluid content container, 1.5rem margins, 1.25rem gutters.
  - Mobile (up to 767px): Fluid single/dual column arrangement, 1rem margins, 1rem gutters.
- **Horizontal Rhythm:** Content-dense listings (e.g., member rosters, investment deals) use `space-sm` and `space-md` gaps. Hero presentation cards and executive profiles expand to `space-lg` and `space-xl` to emphasize exclusivity.
- **Component Padding Standards:**
  - Compact inputs and chips: `space-xs` vertical, `space-sm` horizontal.
  - Cards and data blocks: `space-lg` all around.
  - Modal sheets and primary dialogs: `space-xl` padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined system of hairline borders paired with diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with slate and warm gold.

- **Hairline Borders (0dp Equivalent):**
  - All cards, modules, and persistent containers use a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`). Flat boundaries maintain structure without needing heavy drop shadows.
- **Ambient Floor Elevation (Level 1):**
  - Used for static cards and passive containers:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
- **Raised Elevation (Level 2):**
  - Applied on hover interactions, popovers, and secondary dropdowns:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04);`
- **Executive Float (Level 3):**
  - Reserved for modals, executive profile flyouts, and floating navigation:
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03);`
- **Metallic Glow:**
  - Subtle focus rings and active states use champagne gold:
  - `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 146, 20, 0.18);`

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses the `Soft` standard (`roundedness: 1`), conveying corporate discipline and architectural rigor.

- **Base Components:** Inputs, standard buttons, and small tags use `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Cards & Containers:** Panels, list containers, and standard cards use `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Executive Modals & Sheets:** Floating dialogs, top-level drawers, and action panels use `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Pill Exception:** Status dots, verification seals, and avatar cutouts use fully rounded geometries (`9999px`) to visually contrast against square-edged structural framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Executive:** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#0F172A`. On hover, transitions to background `#1E293B`. Focus uses gold ring (`rgba(198, 146, 20, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Champagne:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#B45309`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. On hover, background transitions to `#FEF3C7` with border `#B45309`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Background `transparent`, text `#334155`. On hover, background transitions to `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F172A`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Verified Member Badge:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border 1px solid rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.2). Uses `caption-caps` font level with an embedded micro metallic icon.
- **Filter Chips:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#64748B`. Selected state: background `#0F172A`, border 1px solid `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards
- **Directory & Metric Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, corner radius `0.5rem`, padding `1.5rem`.
- **Selected / Featured Card:** Adds an inset left border highlight of 2px `#C69214` and Level 2 elevation.

### Input Fields
- **Default State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, height 42px, padding `0 14px`, corner radius `0.25rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#0F172A` with champagne focus glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 146, 20, 0.2)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Unchecked:** 16px square (checkbox) or circle (radio), border 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Checked:** Background `#0F172A`, border `#0F172A`. Checkmark or inner pip rendered in polished `#C69214`.

### Lists & Rosters
- Dividers between rows are strictly 1px hairline `#E2E8F0`.
- Row items maintain vertical padding of `1rem`. Active or hovered rows shift background to `#F8FAFC` with zero latency transitions.

### Verification Shield
- A proprietary UI element across member names: an 18px badge featuring a pure white micro-check inside a dual-tone circular frame of `#C69214` and `#B45309`.
